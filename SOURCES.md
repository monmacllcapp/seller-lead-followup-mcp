# Sources

Every rule in `src/rules/` cites one of these. Checked 2026-10-06.

## Law

- **Contact hours.** 47 CFR 64.1200(c)(1): "No person or entity shall initiate any telephone solicitation to any residential telephone subscriber before the hour of 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. (local time at the called party's location)." <https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200>
- **Opt-outs.** 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(10): consent may be revoked "using any reasonable method"; replying "stop," "quit," "end," "revoke," "opt out," "cancel," or "unsubscribe" to a text revokes it per se. Revocations must be honored within ten business days. Same link.
- **State rules.** JustCall, *State-by-State Mini-TCPA Laws*, updated 2026-09-30. <https://justcall.io/blog/state-mini-tcpa-laws.html>

## Lead response research

- **Lead Response Management study** (James Oldroyd, with InsideSales): calling within 5 minutes instead of 30 raised the odds of contact 100x and of qualifying 21x; 4–6 p.m. was the best contact window. <https://www.mortech.com/hs-fs/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf>
- **HBR, *The Short Life of Online Sales Leads*** (Oldroyd, McElheran, Elkington, 2011): firms that tried to contact a lead within an hour were nearly 7x as likely to qualify it as those that waited even an hour longer, and more than 60x as likely as those that waited 24 hours or more. <https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads>
- **Velocify sales optimization study** (~3.5 million leads): 93% of converted leads were reached by the 6th call; leaving the first voicemail on the 2nd call converted 31% better; two voicemails over six calls converted 34% better than none. <https://www.slideshare.net/Velocify/the-ultimate-contact-strategy>

These studies cover inbound leads across industries, not real estate sellers specifically. They set the inbound speed and attempt counts; they are not used for cold outreach.

## MonmacLabs field protocol

The cadences for cold lists, warm and hot sellers, future timelines, offers and "not interested" come from the calling protocol MonmacLabs runs on live seller outreach. In short:

- Cold list: 7 days, max 4 calls and 1 call a day, texts from the same number, then monthly for 3 months and quarterly after.
- Hot: human call within 5 minutes, same business day at the latest; daily until an appointment or offer.
- Warm: recap text the same day, call within 2–3 business days, weekly for 30 days, then every 2 weeks to monthly.
- Future timeline: follow up 7–14 days before the seller's date.
- Stop immediately on an opt-out, wrong number, complaint or a do-not-call match. No double dialing, no switching caller IDs.

Why the field protocol and not generic research for cold outreach: generic contact studies count attempts on leads who asked to be contacted. Cold sellers did not, so the protocol holds back to one call a day, which also keeps numbers off carrier spam lists and inside the state caps above.

## Not used

The common claim that "80% of deals close after the 5th contact" has no traceable original study, so no rule here depends on it.
